# Welcome to the InnerSource Working Group! 🎉

## Quick 10-Min Intro

The InnerSource Working Group is a group of practitioners who help each other make internal code collaboration work at full organization scale.

<!--

## Who This Is For 🤝

**Anyone doing InnerSource in their organization**

- 🏢 InnerSource or Open Source Program Offices
- 🌱 Company communities of practice
- 🧑‍💻 Individuals driving InnerSource without a formal program

**Any stage** - just starting out, scaling up, or keeping it healthy.

<!--
A quick word on who this is for, because we changed this recently.

We used to be the ISPO Working Group, built around InnerSource Program Offices. That name
turned away people who were doing exactly this work without a program office to point at,
and that was never what we wanted. So we're the InnerSource Working Group now, and the
focus is the practitioner rather than the org chart.

If you're in a program office, you're still exactly who we're here for. And so is the
person running a community of practice, and the engineer quietly pushing this uphill at a
company that has never used the word InnerSource. Wherever you are on that road, starting
out or years in, you'll find people here a step ahead of you and a step behind you.

## Two Ways In 🚪

**InnerSource Community Call** - the garden 🌿
Open, exploratory, no commitment. Come listen, ask, meet people.

**InnerSource Working Group** - the house 🏠
Structured working sessions. Come build things with us.

<!--
There are two doors into this community, and they're deliberately different.

The InnerSource Community Call is the garden. It's open, it's exploratory, and it asks
nothing of you. Show up, listen, ask the question you've been chewing on, meet a few
people. No commitment at all.

The InnerSource Working Group is the house. This is where we roll up our sleeves in
structured working sessions and actually build the things. If you want to contribute to a
project and work alongside people on it, this is your room.

Most people start in the garden and wander into the house when something catches their
interest. That's exactly how it's meant to go, and there's no wrong order.
-->

- 🎓 Learning materials

We build them together, in the open, in our biweekly sessions.

## How to Get Involved 🎯

**Three easy ways:**

1. **Join our Slack** → `#innersource-working-group` in InnerSource Commons
2. **Attend a meeting** → We'll post the next one in Slack
3. **Add a challenge** → Share what you're working on via our Kanban board

**Ready to contribute?** We'd love to have you! 🎊
