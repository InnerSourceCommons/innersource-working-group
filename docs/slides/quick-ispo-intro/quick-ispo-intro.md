# Welcome to the InnerSource Working Group! 🎉

## Quick 10-Min Intro
 
The InnerSource Working Group (ISWG) is a group of people who are interested in improving the internal code collaboration experience at full organization scale.

## What We Do 🚀

**We build InnerSource resources together!**

- 📚 Patterns & Practices
- 📖 Managing InnerSource Projects book
- 🎓 Learning materials
- 🛠️ Tools & templates

**How?** Through **attending our biweekly meetings** and **contributing to our Kanban board**.

## How to Get Involved 🎯

**Three easy ways:**

1. **Join our Slack** → `#innersource-working-group` in InnerSource Commons
2. **Attend a meeting** → We'll post the next one in Slack
3. **Add a challenge** → Share what you're working on via our Kanban board

**Ready to contribute?** We'd love to have you! 🎊
